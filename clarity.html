Clarity – A Health and Wellness Focused App Concept

Team Member
Clarity is a concept application designed during the Fall of 2019 by a group of four University of Southern California students. Clarity’s mission was to demystify the world of product labeling in the food and beverage, household products, and cosmetics space.
My role on the team focused on developing user stories and personas and developing features to meet the needs of these users. I also led the build-out of the app prototype using Figma.
