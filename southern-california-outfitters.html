Southern California Outfitters
Finance Director and Backcountry Guide
SC Outfitters is a 100% student-run outdoor guiding service at the University of Southern California. The mission is simple, connect as many students as possible with the wilderness of Southern California, the West, and beyond.
During my time with SC Outfitters, I led cycling trips around Los Angeles, surfing trips in Malibu, 4x4 excursions in the Owen’s River Valley, and hikes in the High Sierra. I was also responsible for all club finances. In December 2019, I obtained my Wilderness First Responder certification through an 8-day intensive backcountry medicine course.
